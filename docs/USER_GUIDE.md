# The System — User Guide

This guide explains the app without requiring technical knowledge. The same essential information is available inside the app through the **Help** button in the top-right corner.

## What The System is

The System is a private, phone-first progression game built around real-life actions. You complete missions that matter to you, and the app records the resulting XP, stats, levels, streaks, challenges, achievements, and ranks.

The game language makes progress easier to see. It does not measure your worth, diagnose you, or decide whether a day was meaningful.

Your data stays in the browser on your device unless you export a backup. The app has no login, remote AI, analytics, advertisements, or tracking.

## The simplest way to use it

1. Open **Missions**.
2. Choose one objective that is realistic today.
3. Complete it only when it is genuinely done.
4. Return to **System** to see the recorded progress.
5. Use the **Party Channel** whenever you want emotional or practical support.

You do not need to use every screen every day.

## Where everything lives

### System

Your main dashboard. It shows today's completion, rank, account level, System state, quick missions, current challenge, recent stat activity, rare events, and your companion roster.

### Missions

Your full objective list. Missions can use a simple completion button or request details such as time, quantity, checklist items, or a creator-work path. Optional missions award XP without deciding Perfect Day status.

### Status

Your progression record: overall level, rank qualification, individual stats, titles, achievements, cosmetics, and collection progress.

### Challenges

Longer arcs such as weekly, monthly, Boss, Recovery, and Rank Trial challenges. Challenges have explicit requirements and saved progress.

### Party Channel

The companions' dedicated space. It contains:

- **Check-In:** Choose the mood that feels closest. Snow opens and closes while the whole party responds.
- **Direct Support:** Choose Motivation, Make a plan, Faith & perspective, Calm down, Recover, or Celebrate. Then choose the whole party or one specific companion.
- **Saved:** Revisit companion messages you marked with a heart.

Check-Ins and Direct Support are private, work offline, and never change XP, missions, streaks, rewards, or penalties.

### Archive

Your permanent record. It contains daily reviews, mission history, stat transactions, level and rank history, reports, rare signals, Party Check-Ins, Direct Support channels, favorites, banter, and companion transmissions.

### Settings

Appearance, companion frequency, individual companion controls, mission configuration, Recovery Mode, privacy, sound, vibration, backups, import, and reset tools.

### About & Help

Tap **Help** in the top-right corner from any page. Snow explains the app, gives a three-step starting path, and links directly to every major destination.

## XP, levels, stats, and ranks

**Account XP** raises your overall level. **Stat XP** records which areas of life a mission strengthened. Each mission shows its rewards before completion.

Ranks are separate from levels. A rank requires balanced long-term evidence such as account level, completed missions, recorded days, Discipline, balanced stats, challenge completions, and sometimes a Rank Trial.

XP is written through stable transaction IDs so repeated taps cannot award the same mission twice. Undo is available only during the active System day and writes an inverse transaction instead of deleting history.

## The companion party

- **Snow, The Constant:** Your primary whole-journey companion.
- **Rook, The Vanguard:** Strength, Endurance, and Vitality.
- **Selah, The Beacon:** Faith, Wisdom, and spiritual consistency.
- **Cipher, The Strategist:** Discipline, Focus, Willpower, Creativity, YouTube, and ARC.
- **Haven, The Guardian:** Character, Empathy, Recovery, and comebacks.

Ordinary reactions follow the companion frequency selected in Settings. Party Banter is an occasional two-character exchange after a mission. Major ranks, level milestones, and achievements can trigger a full-party celebration.

Tap the heart beside a companion message to place a copy in **Words to Carry**. Removing the heart deletes only the saved copy—not campaign history.

## Rare events and Mission Passes

Each System day receives one saved rare-event roll. Refreshing the app cannot reroll it.

- An optional **Emergency Quest** can be accepted or declined without penalty.
- A **Mission Pass** can protect one pending required mission without using a monthly protected exception. It does not award that mission's XP.
- Most days have no rare event.

## Hard days, missed days, and recovery

A missed mission or broken streak remains honestly visible, but it does not erase levels or permanently lock the journey.

Useful options include:

- Open **Party Channel → Direct Support → Recover**.
- Activate **Recovery Mode** in Settings.
- Excuse a mission when the situation genuinely qualifies.
- Use a saved Mission Pass.
- Reduce the day to one realistic action and return tomorrow.

The app is not medical care, crisis support, pastoral care, or a substitute for trusted people. If you feel unsafe or need human help, contact someone you trust or an appropriate local support service.

## Appearance and phone use

The System interface is the more game-like presentation. Clean reduces decorative effects. Abyss is the dark theme; Daybreak uses a light gray, navy, and sun-yellow palette. Visual intensity and reduced motion can be adjusted separately.

For the intended full-screen phone experience, open the GitHub Pages address in Safari and choose **Share → Add to Home Screen**.

## Backups and changing phones

Your progress is not stored in GitHub. It lives in the browser data on the current device.

Before clearing browser data, changing phones, or installing a major release:

1. Open **Settings → Local Data**.
2. Choose **Export save**.
3. Store the downloaded JSON file somewhere private and recoverable.
4. On the new installation, choose **Import save** and review the preview before confirming.

Backups include Party conversations, favorite messages, banter history, settings, progression, missions, and the rest of the campaign record.

## Updating the installed app

After a new version is pushed to GitHub and the Pages workflow succeeds, open the installed app while online. Accept the update prompt when it appears. Existing data is migrated locally rather than replaced.

Exporting a backup before a major update is still the safest habit.
